# FundPath business model

## What FundPath is, and is not

FundPath helps people apply for a Constituency Development Fund (CDF) loan and plan how they
will repay it if it is granted. It is **not the lender**. The council and the CDF Committee
decide and disburse. FundPath adds the stage the published application route does not cover:
repayment support (schedule, balance, reminders) and one clear view for the council.

## Why it matters (figures as reported, see sources)

- CDF per constituency rose from K1.6m (2021) to K40m (2026); 156 constituencies, about K6.24bn.
- In 2025, loans were K421m for 6,694 beneficiaries (Zambia Monitor, 27 Jan 2026).
- By Sept 2026, about 35% of loans made since 2021 had been repaid, and the 2026 loan
  component was suspended (Lusaka Times, 29 Sep 2026). A Jan 2026 briefing cited about 9%.
- Illustrative arithmetic: each 10-point gain in recovery on K421m is about K42.1m returned to
  revolving pools. This is not a forecast.

## Where it adds value (value chain)

Marketing and discovery of funding windows; appraisal (pre-scored, complete applications);
service after disbursement (repayment schedule and reminders). It lowers transaction costs
(search and coordination).

## Customers and revenue

- Realistic payers: councils, the Ministry or donors. Borrowers are low income and are not the
  payer.
- Revenue: a council subscription (156 constituencies) or a share of improved recovery;
  secondary income from advisor commissions and storefront fees.
- Positioning: consultancy-led first ("loan readiness and recovery support" delivered with the
  platform), then a platform subscription once councils rely on the dashboard.

## Pilot and risks

Pilot in one constituency; measure application quality and on-time repayment.
Risks: council adoption; scoring rules are prototype rules and not validated; no real users yet;
FundPath cannot force repayment.

## Possible AI features (not built)

Business-plan coach; repayment early warning (rules first, trained models only once real data
exists); plain-language and local-language help by SMS or WhatsApp; admin question assistant;
duplicate checks. People make every lending decision.
