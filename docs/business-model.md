# FundPath business model

## What FundPath is, and is not

FundPath is an **information service**. It helps people apply for a Constituency Development
Fund (CDF) loan and plan how they will repay it, and it gives small sellers a marketplace.
It is **not the lender**. The CDF committee shortlists, a bank appraises, the Provincial Local
Government Officer approves and the bank disburses (draft CDF Guidelines, April 2025).

## The problem

- CDF per constituency rose from K1.6m (2021) to K40m (2026); 156 constituencies, about K6.24bn.
- In 2025, loans were K421m for 6,694 beneficiaries (Zambia Monitor, 27 Jan 2026).
- By Sept 2026, about 35% of loans made since 2021 had been repaid, and the 2026 loan
  component was suspended (Lusaka Times, 29 Sep 2026). A Jan 2026 briefing cited about 9%.
- Applicants decide with poor information: windows are hard to find, there is no easy way to
  test a plan or the size of a request, and loan terms are explained late. The Ministry asked
  councils to explain loan terms before disbursement (Zambia Monitor, 12 May 2025).
- Small sellers lack information on demand and prices.

We do not claim to know why borrowers default. Poor information is one plausible cause that
FundPath can address; it is a hypothesis to test in a pilot.

## Value proposition: information as a service

| For | Information delivered | In the prototype |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applicants | Open opportunities by constituency; real loan terms; 0-100 feasibility score; advisor guidance; repayment schedule and balance | Built (demo data) |
| Marketplace | Verified seller listings and buyer discovery; market assessment of a business plan | Listings built; assessment is rule-based |

## Customers and revenue

- Applicants and business owners pay for information they use: a K150 plan, risk and market
  assessment, and paid advisor sessions (15% commission).
- Marketplace sales carry an 8% fee, paid by the seller out of the sale.
- Applying through FundPath is free. These rates are assumptions, not agreed terms.
- Possible later extension: councils, the Ministry or donors license the admin dashboard.
  Councils buy under the Public Procurement Act, so this is a slow sale and is not part of
  the core proposition.

## Pilot and risks

Pilot in one constituency; measure application quality and on-time repayment.
Risks: applicants may not pay for information they can get free from a ward office; scoring
rules are prototype rules and not validated; no real users yet; better information does not
guarantee repayment.

## Possible AI features (not built)

Business-plan coach; repayment early warning (rules first, trained models only once real data
exists); plain-language and local-language help by SMS or WhatsApp; duplicate checks. People
make every lending decision.
